- Current feature working progress:
[ ] - Increase the number of drawng tha you make. Change how you do redo, undo. You have to make it be 
      something like a queue or textures that you draw in. 
      So you will have the initial last state for the texture that you have. 
      It will be empty at first. If you have 5 draw record max and you draw the 6th, the oldest 1 will
      get drawing into the intial state texture and the new one will be the new 5th. So for static they work the same,
      you just have to compose them each frame, should be fine. 
      For faded textures, you would have to have them be updated and then also composed in.
      Those will get removed from the queue when they fade out. If you have 5 max and the olders 1 is the faded one,
      but it is still fading and you draw a new one, you just remove the oldest one. This is not the clenest way, sure,
      but if you get to have enought space for the list of texture to never be filled up, then you shoud be fine.

      - You could just have 50 textures and compose all 50 each frame. 
        If this is too slow for some reason, then just have the draw record store the old and the new texture like 
        right now, but just dont use them for faded, since they get composed in. This will allow you to not have to deal
        with 50 textures each frame when you do regular drawings.
